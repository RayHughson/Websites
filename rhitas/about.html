About RHITAS

Independent Technology Assurance.

RH IT & Security Ltd is an independent technology assurance consultancy, helping organisations understand the relationship between their technology, security, risk and business objectives.

We work at the point where technical complexity meets business accountability providing an independent view of how technology environments are governed, controlled and managed.

Bridging Technology and Assurance

Technology and security are increasingly inseparable from business risk, yet the technical detail can make it difficult for decision-makers to see the bigger picture.

Our role is to bridge that gap.

We assess technology environments, security controls, governance arrangements and supporting evidence to establish where an organisation stands today, what matters most and where attention should be focused.

The emphasis is not simply on identifying weaknesses. It is on understanding their significance and turning technical findings into clear, prioritised information that supports informed decisions.

Areas of assurance

  • Infrastructure assurance
  • Cyber security assessment and readiness
  • Security assurance and control assessment
  • Cyber Essentials readiness

  • Vulnerability and remediation assurance

A Different Perspective

Technical understanding with business context.

Independent judgement

RHITAS provides an objective view of technology, security and risk without being tied to a particular product, vendor or implementation approach.

Technical depth

Effective assurance requires more than reviewing policies and evidence. We understand the underlying technology well enough to question what the evidence actually demonstrates.

Business relevance

Technical findings only matter when their significance is understood. We translate complex issues into clear, proportionate information that supports business decisions.

Working principle

Make the technology position understandable.

Good assurance should make it easier for decision-makers to understand where they stand, what matters most and what needs attention. The objective isn't assurance for its own sake; it is clear, evidence-based understanding that supports proportionate risk reduction and informed business decisions.
