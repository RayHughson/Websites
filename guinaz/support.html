Support & Upgrades

Have an existing Guinaz system?

The answer is often not “replace everything”. For compatible Digital installations, Tactile can provide a modern route forward.

Premier Digital

Compatible installations can often be upgraded to Tactile monitors while retaining the existing system architecture.

Upgrade path

Premier → Tactile

If a Premier monitor has failed or needs replacing, the first question is whether the existing installation is compatible with the Tactile range.

01 · Identify

What do you have?

Model numbers, entrance-panel photographs and existing monitor details can help identify the system.

02 · Check

Establish compatibility

Not every historic installation is identical, so compatibility should be confirmed before ordering replacement equipment.

03 · Upgrade

Tactile

Where compatible, a Tactile monitor can provide a modern replacement without treating the whole installation as obsolete.

04 · Support

Know the limits

Older analogue systems and discontinued equipment may have limited support or no replacement route.

Legacy analogue systems

Guinaz analogue products have been discontinued for many years. Limited technical support may still be possible, but replacement product availability cannot generally be guaranteed.

DECT & telephone integration

Earlier Guinaz systems could integrate door entry with conventional telephone and DECT equipment. With the UK's transition away from PSTN, the original telephone service is no longer a straightforward supported solution.

In some circumstances, SIP and analogue telephone adapters can provide a route to integrate legacy equipment with modern telephone services. These solutions are more involved and are considered case-by-case rather than as a standard replacement product.
