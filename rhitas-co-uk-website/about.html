About RHITAS

Independent Technology Assurance.

RH IT & Security Ltd is an independent technology assurance consultancy, helping organisations understand their technology landscape, evaluate meaningful risks and make informed decisions with confidence.

Bridging Technology and Assurance

Effective assurance requires more than understanding frameworks or reviewing documentation. It requires enough technical understanding to interrogate the evidence, understand how technology operates in practice and recognise the implications when controls, processes or governance fall short.

RHITAS brings together technical understanding and business assurance, translating complex findings into clear, proportionate and actionable recommendations.

How we work

Assurance built on evidence, not assumption.

Independent Thinking

Objective assessment without product, vendor or implementation bias.

Evidence Before Assumption

Conclusions supported by demonstrable evidence rather than perception or assumption.

Practical Assurance

Recommendations that are proportionate, achievable and aligned with organisational priorities.

Working principle

Make the technology position understandable.

Good assurance should make it easier for decision-makers to understand where they stand, what matters most and what needs attention. The objective isn't assurance for its own sake; it is clear, evidence-based understanding that supports proportionate risk reduction and informed business decisions.
